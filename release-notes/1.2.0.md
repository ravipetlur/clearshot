- **Updates inside the app.** ClearShot checks for a new version once a day, or when you choose Check for Updates… in the menu bar, shows what's new, and installs it when you say so. Every download is checked before it replaces the app: its checksum, Apple's notarization, and that it's signed by the same developer as the copy you're running. This is the last version you install by hand.
- **Sensitive info check.** After a screenshot, a badge on the thumbnail flags email addresses, phone numbers, API keys and tokens, private keys, passwords after a label, card numbers and IP addresses. **Mask All** pixelates them in one click (and updates ClearShot's copy on the clipboard), **Undo Mask** takes them off, and **Review in Annotate…** opens the capture so you can check each one.
- **In Annotate,** a button in the tool strip shows how many possible secrets the picture has, masks them in one click, and unmasks them again (Edit › Mask Sensitive Info does the same).
- **Settings › Quick Access › Sensitive info** turns the automatic check on or off and chooses what to look for.
- Everything is checked on your Mac; nothing it finds leaves it or is logged. It's a help, not a guarantee, so look before you share.
- Exports draw text the same way whatever your Mac's font smoothing setting.
