- **Measure and sample the picture in Annotate.**
  - **Colours:** Tab copies the colour under the pointer, ⇧Tab the text colour, and ⌥Tab a selection's average, as HEX, `rgb()`, `hsl()` or `oklch()`.
  - **Ruler:** hold an arrow key to measure to the nearest edge, then click to keep the measurement on the picture.
  - **Selecting:** ⌘-click selects a flat area, and ⌥A fits a selection to its content.
  - **Contrast and guides:** a selection shows its contrast (WCAG 2, or APCA with ⌥-click). ⌥S and ⌥D show guides.
  - **Units:** sizes can show in points or pixels.
- **Magnifier (M).** A zoomed callout of part of the picture, 2× to 8×, round or rounded square, joined to the spot it magnifies. It never shows what a redaction hides or what you cropped away.
- **Erase.** A new redaction style that fills an area from the pixels around it, so a stray icon or name simply disappears. Select an area and press Delete to erase it.
- **Text only redaction.** Pixelate, blur, black out or erase just the words in an area, and leave the background between them.
- **Shapes and arrows.**
  - Rectangles and ellipses can be filled.
  - Every arrow can bend from its middle handle.
  - A Hand-drawn style gives shapes, arrows and text a sketched look. ⇧⌘R draws a new variation.
- **Pasted pictures** have their own opacity (keys 1–9 and 0), and File › Export Before/After GIF… makes a two-frame GIF without and with the top picture.
- **Precise selections.** While capturing an area, hold Z for a 10× zoom around the pointer, hold ⌘ to slow the pointer, and use W A S D to move it one point at a time. In Annotate, the arrow keys move a selection, ⌘-arrows resize it, and ⌘2 zooms to it.
- **Automatic file format.** Settings can save PNG for screenshots of text and interfaces and JPEG for photos, choosing for each picture.
- **Projects using any of the new tools** are saved in a newer format. Older versions of ClearShot can't open those projects and say so, instead of dropping objects.
- **Fixed:** the canvas colour and background auto-balance could pick up colours from under a redaction. Erase and Text only are built so that nothing hidden can show through.
